# Items · #FT000001
> ⚙ #FT000001V1.0 · V1.0 released

## Description & Requirements
Members need one dependable place for the things they keep track of.

In scope: finding, creating and changing items. Out of scope: sharing items with other people.

## ⚙ Sub-features
- [#FT000003 Browse items](ft000003-browse-items.md) — Lets a Member find an item in their list.
- [#FT000004 Edit an item](ft000004-edit-an-item.md) — Lets a Member create an item or change its details.
